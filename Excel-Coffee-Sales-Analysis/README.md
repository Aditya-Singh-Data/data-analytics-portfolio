# ☕ Coffee Sales Data Analysis Project

An end-to-end data analysis project using **Microsoft Excel** to analyze global coffee transactions, track regional performance, and uncover customer purchasing trends.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this project was to analyze raw coffee sales data spanning across multiple international regions to evaluate total revenue generation, product preferences, and customer distribution patterns using advanced Excel features.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Microsoft Excel**
  * **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Formatting order dates, validating customer records, and standardizing product categories.
  * **Data Analysis & Aggregation:** Pivot Tables, XLOOKUP, and Statistical Functions.
  * **Data Visualization:** Interactive Charts, Slicers, and a professional summary dashboard.

---

## 📊 Dataset Summary & Key Metrics
* **Total Transactions:** 1,000 coffee orders processed and analyzed.
* **Total Sales Revenue:** $45,134.26 generated across the dataset.
* **Geographical Reach:** Sales recorded across three major regions:
  * **United States:** 774 orders
  * **Ireland:** 153 orders
  * **United Kingdom:** 73 orders
* **Coffee Varieties Analyzed:** Arabica, Liberica, Excelsa, and Robusta.

---

## 💡 Key Findings & Insights
* **Top-Selling Coffee Type:** Arabica led the product categories with 264 total orders, closely followed by Liberica, Excelsa, and Robusta variants.
* **Regional Dominance:** The United States market constitutes the vast majority of total sales volume (~77.4% of total order transactions).
* **Customer Segmentation:** Integrated customer loyalty data to examine purchasing behaviors between regular and loyalty card holders.
